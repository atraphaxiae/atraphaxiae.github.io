Procrastinated a lot on this review. Mostly because I finished doing Liyue like 20 days ago, and I'm
now two out of five subregions complete in Inazuma. Genshin suddenly feels so enjoyable after
getting out of the Mondstadt purgatory that I used to find myself in. I wonder why that is...

Anyways, Liyue. Personally, I'm a big fan of Liyue, but not the main region itself. I find myself
liking the Chasm and Chenyu Vale a lot more than regular old Liyue, though probably this is an
effect of me having completed this region like three times already, which is two times more than
what should be normal for me. At this point Liyue just feels bland, no offense to our Chinese
players. Everything's basically just a "go there, press 'F' a few times, kill a few enemies, quest
done". I mean you can certainly say that about the entirety of Genshin, but Liyue does it in a way
that there's little replayability. That's unlike the Chasm, which I'm excited to re-experience
despite already completing it twice.

But I'd say that Liyue is generally an improvement over Mondstadt. When I first played the game
casually and without map handholding, I was amazed with just how many things there are in Liyue.
Genshin was the first open-world RPG I've ever played, and Liyue was an incredible experience, even
with the constant FPS drops and crashes on my old fugly trash Acer Nitro 5 "gaming" laptop. I swear,
that laptop cost me a decade of my life.

Of course, not everything is just sunshine and rainbows over here in Liyue. This region is literally
atrocious climbing central. It would've helped if I was an incel who used Lisa for everything
climbing-related, but I've got a lot of interactive map alt-tabbing to do, and I can't waste it on
earthly and beastly things like that. The 100% grind is a spiritual one, and every gilded subregion
completion percent takes me one step closer to the Heavenly Principles.

I also hate the fact that you get Anemo and Geo BS everywhere in the map. I had to switch my party
up and build Fischl to replace CMC because I had to re-resonate my CMC to become GMC because of
these annoying puzzles. And I have it on easy mode right now; imagine being that F2P 1.0 newcomer
(me in the past) that only had Traveller as their sole Anemo and Geo character, re-re-resonating
from Geo to Anemo and re-re-re-resonating back every time you see one of those frustrating
windmills. If you're also frustrated by my repetition of the "re"-s, that's how it re-re-re-re-feels
every time I have to sit through that annoying unskippable animation when I'm
re-re-re-re-re-resonating my MC, who, by the way, is SUPPOSED to be able to wield all the elements
at once. Almost a decade of Genshin goodness and that burden of a cutscene is still unskippable. Not
that there should even be a cutscene in the first place; this kind of thing should literally just be
a single button in the menu. But the Hoyo gods withholdeth, and withholdeth again, and withholdeth
again again.

My point is that Hoyo seems to love withholding QoL updates to their literal biggest cash-cow. My
other non-tangential but less-important point is that Liyue adds a lot of things; half are annoying
and half are enjoyable. Maybe I glossed over those enjoyable things in this review, but that's what
happens when you complete the game a million times and forget the good parts because you've already
experienced them a billion times, and what's left is the annoyance of completing the game again.

But anyways, considering the "annoyance bias" that I have right now, I'll consider my past feelings
for Liyue more in this rating. I'd give Liyue a **4/5**. It's not my favorite region either, but
it's an improvement over Mondstadt in the exploration quality and quantity. However, a lot of those
new things are just outright annoying to do. Next up is Inazuma, which is the region I should be
dogging on right now. Unexpectedly, I'm actually enjoying it for some reason. More on this after I
complete Inazuma and procrastinate writing a review again by moving on to the Chasm.
