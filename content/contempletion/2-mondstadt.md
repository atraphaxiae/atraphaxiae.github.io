I've had an on and off hatred for Genshin Impact ever since I started playing at launch. I loved it
because of the exploration, I hated it because of the egregious pulling mechanics which gave me a
C6 Qiqi and a C6 Kaeya on my first account. Oh how the times change; Qiqi is now an S-rank character
because of the new super-shilled Stellar-Conduct and Stellar-Swirl reactions. Maybe I should go back
to that account?

Well, this is my fifth Genshin account now. I've finally found my favorite way to play the game,
which is to not play it properly. Instead, I have the unofficial interactive map open at the same
time, and I play the game solely for map completion. I don't care about my characters, I only care
about that sweet golden 100% you see when you complete a region in Teyvat.

Of course, the first nation should be the first one you complete, which is why I did a 100% map
completion for Mondstadt first. For various dumb reasons, this is the fifth time I've done this same
thing, which means I have Mondstadt 100%-ed on all of the five accounts I have. For this completion,
I only considered the initial Mondstadt map, so I didn't include Dragonspine, Windrest Peak and the
Temple of Space. Probably the most annoying area was Stormterror's Lair, with the infuriating amount
of chests on cliffs and anemoculi on cliffs and other stuff on cliffs. It's only redeemed by its
music, which is my favorite across all of Mondstadt, though the entirety of Mondstadt has some great
music.

This completion was completely carried by both Yumemizuki Mizuki and Cryo Traveler, which allowed me
to use Stellar-Swirl all the time. It felt like cheating, since I've never experienced Genshin with
an actually somewhat-optimal team, which made everything super easy-breezy. I remember the times
when I struggled with the whole game because I was using Noelle as a main DPS since I had no one
else. When you take 10 minutes just to kill a single ruin guard, the game gets super tiring. Thank
God for the Snezhnaya update actually giving you the most meta team right at the start.

Anyways, I think Mondstadt gets a **4/5** from me. It has the easiest completion out of all regions,
but I wouldn't say that it's my favorite one. There might also be some nostalgia bias in this rating
since Mondstadt is the very first nation you explore. Also, please don't do what I'm doing. I think
I'm the only person who enjoys playing Genshin wrong like this. Just enjoy the lore and the
exploration like a normal person.
